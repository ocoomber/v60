First-time setup

Turn on the scale. If its WiFi is already set up, tap Pair scale and choose CoffeeScale.

Otherwise, join CoffeeScale-Setup in your phone’s WiFi settings, then tap Set up scale WiFi. Choose your home WiFi. Connect your phone back to that WiFi, return here and tap Pair scale.

Allow Bluetooth for pairing and local network access for weighing. After that, just turn on the scale and open this app.

To keep this app: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen → Install.
